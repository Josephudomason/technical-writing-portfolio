# Account Management

> NovaBank is a fictional banking application used for portfolio documentation. It is not a real bank, financial institution, or regulated product.

## View Your Balance

The account dashboard shows available balance, current balance, and recent account activity. Available balance may differ from current balance when pending transactions exist.

## View Account Information

Open **Accounts**, choose an account, and select **Account details** to view account type, masked account number, routing information, and account status.

## Manage Account Details

Editable profile information lives under [Profile Settings](profile-settings.md). Sensitive account identifiers cannot be edited by users.

## Best Practices

- Review balances before starting a transfer.
- Report unfamiliar account activity immediately.
- Keep contact information current so alerts reach you.
