# Security

> NovaBank is a fictional banking application used for portfolio documentation. It is not a real bank, financial institution, or regulated product.

## Password Management

Use a unique password and update it if you suspect exposure. NovaBank never asks for your full password in support messages.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication from **Security settings**. Authenticator apps are generally more reliable than SMS when traveling or changing phone numbers.

## Device Management

Review trusted devices regularly. Remove devices you no longer use or do not recognize.

## Suspicious Activity

If you see unfamiliar transactions, password reset emails, or login alerts, change your password, remove unknown devices, and contact support through the official app.
