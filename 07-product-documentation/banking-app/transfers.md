# Transfers

> NovaBank is a fictional banking application used for portfolio documentation. It is not a real bank, financial institution, or regulated product.

## Make a Transfer

1. Open **Transfers**.
2. Choose the source account.
3. Choose or add a recipient.
4. Enter the amount and optional memo.
5. Review fees, timing, and limits.
6. Confirm the transfer.

## Transfer Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Transfer has been submitted but not completed |
| Completed | Funds were sent successfully |
| Failed | Transfer did not complete |
| Canceled | Transfer was canceled before processing |

## Failed Transfers

Transfers may fail because of insufficient funds, incorrect recipient details, limit restrictions, verification issues, or temporary service problems.

## Transfer Limits

Limits may vary by account type, verification level, recipient, and transfer method. NovaBank displays applicable limits before confirmation.
