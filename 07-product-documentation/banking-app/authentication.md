# Authentication

> NovaBank is a fictional banking application used for portfolio documentation. It is not a real bank, financial institution, or regulated product.

## Login Requirements

NovaBank requires an email address, password, and any enabled second factor. A second factor may be an authenticator app code, SMS code, or approved device prompt.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be unique, difficult to guess, and not reused from other services. The fictional product requires at least 12 characters.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second proof of identity after the password. Enable it from [Security](security.md).

## Failed Login Attempts

Several failed attempts may temporarily lock login to protect the account. Use password reset or contact support if the lock persists.
